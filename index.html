Plot Twist: Sunflower Puzzle

A calm logic puzzle. Plant one sunflower in every row, column and colored plot, and never let two touch.

Support

Questions, bug reports or feedback? Open an issue at github.com/GranTurismo/plottwist-site/issues and we will get back to you.

How to play

  • Plant exactly one sunflower in every row, every column and every colored plot.
  • Two sunflowers can never touch, not even diagonally.
  • Tap once to mark a square empty, tap twice to plant, drag to mark many squares.
  • Every puzzle has one answer and can be solved by logic alone.
