Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Plot Twist: Sunflower Puzzle does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data.

  • The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no third-party SDKs.
  • The app makes no network requests. Fonts and all game content are bundled inside the app.
  • Your settings (sound, auto-mark), your daily result and your streak are saved only on your device and are removed when you delete the app.
  • If you choose "Share result", the share text is handed to the system share sheet, and only you decide where it goes.

Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
